Web App Case Study & Demo

Mobile Book Tracking in The Stacks

Explore a lightweight Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to help users track their reading habits, manage book collections, and discover new titles. This demo showcases a bespoke mobile-first user experience designed for coziness and data storytelling.

About this Project

My wife needed to replace her ancient book-tracking app, but nothing she could find met her needs. So I built a Progressive Web App (PWA) called The Stacks to fit her exact requirements. The app is designed to be lightweight and cozy, optimized for her to wander through her reading journey, not to track pages, chase goals, or bog her down in other unwanted features.

Requirements

A lightweight book-tracking app with cozy aesthetics and streamlined featureset. No social features, goals, or fine-grained tracking. Must include a dynamic library, scan-to-search functionality, and tracking by month, year, and all time.

The Solution

A mobile-first PWA that can be installed on any device for a native app experience without the overhead of a traditional mobile app. The first version fits my wife's needs perfectly, including a few delightful moments, such as the opening animation and Focus view.

Install on Your Device

This app is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA). Access the link directly on your phone and tap Add to Home Screen to install it for a full screen native app experience.
